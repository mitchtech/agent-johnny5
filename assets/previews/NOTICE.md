# Preview images

The waveform GIFs, pet animation GIFs, social card, README banner, and touch
icon are generated by [`scripts/build-assets.py`](../../scripts/build-assets.py)
from the committed clips in [`audio/`](../../audio) and the pet atlas in
[`pets/johnny5`](../../pets/johnny5). The waveforms depict *Short Circuit* audio,
whose rights remain with their owners; see the [audio rights notice](../../audio/NOTICE.md).

Pet frames, and the robot on the social card and banner, reproduce the Johnny 5
pet artwork by Michael J. Mitchell / mitchtech under
[CC BY 4.0](../../pets/johnny5/LICENSE.txt). The GIFs place unscaled atlas
cells on a background; the cards scale a single frame. See the [artwork notice](../../pets/johnny5/NOTICE.md)
for provenance. Third-party character rights remain with their owners.

The generation code is MIT licensed. Card lettering uses the OFL-licensed fonts
listed in [site fonts](../../site/fonts/OFL.md). No affiliation with the rights
holders is implied.
