# Johnny 5 pet artwork

The Johnny 5 Codex pet is not bundled yet. This directory holds the manifest
only, so the installer, validation, and release packaging are ready for it.

Until the art ships, `agent-johnny5 pet install` reports that the pet is not
bundled and changes nothing. Codex's native pet feature can create a pet in the
meantime.

When the art lands, add `spritesheet.webp` (8 x 11 atlas of 192 x 208 cells,
Codex sprite version 2), `LICENSE.txt` for the artwork, and its provenance here.
Validation and packaging enable themselves once the sprite sheet exists.

Johnny 5 and *Short Circuit* belong to their respective rights holders. This
project is not affiliated with or endorsed by them.
